This repository is a hybrid reading index.

- Nine unmodified PDFs are redistributed under their own file-level Creative Commons licenses. Their authors, canonical sources, license URLs, and public filenames are listed in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](./THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
- Seven other full texts are linked from official author, publisher, arXiv, or institutional-repository endpoints and are not copied into this repository or its Zotero package.
- The Doyle OCR derivative is local-only. Only the official MIT DSpace scan is linked.
- The reading notes are editorial paraphrases, evidence boundaries, questions, and explicitly labeled inferences produced during a human-machine collaborative reading workflow. Every entry states its actual `noteDepth` and reading scope; `read` is used only where the richer evidence layers have actually been prepared. No entry may be represented as a source quotation, a complete reading beyond its stated scope, a reproduced experiment, or an author claim.
- Public research tests must be paper-facing, system-agnostic, independently understandable, and based on public or synthetic fixtures. Each states method, environment, controls, raw versus derived results, limitations, and a reproducible artifact link. The Doyle static oracle is a bounded editorial reconstruction, not the original TMS implementation or a reproduction of the paper.
- The public site does not publish private project mappings, source-inspection notes, runtime or offline probes, internal revision identifiers, prompts, sessions, routing evidence, private names, local paths, or private continuity.
- The reader is static and contains no backend. Its only third-party runtime script is the Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon, used for aggregate traffic and performance measurement without cookies, localStorage, visitor profiles, query-string collection, or custom reading events.
- No open-source or Creative Commons license is granted for the reader code or editorial notes at this time. Public visibility does not by itself grant permission to reproduce or adapt them.

Its grant covers that PDF and its four Faye-controlled project screenshots only; attribution and source details are recorded in [publications/README.md](./publications/README.md).

The file-level paper licenses and the selected project-publication license do not license this repository as a whole. Links are provided for scholarship and navigation. If a source, access label, or attribution is incorrect, please open an issue with the canonical source.
